Your haemoglobin level tells us how much of a special protein is inside your red blood cells.

This protein, called haemoglobin, is what actually carries oxygen. Think of red blood cells as vehicles and haemoglobin as the fuel inside them. Without enough haemoglobin, your body cannot carry oxygen properly.

This is one of the most important numbers in your blood report. It is measured because low haemoglobin, also called anaemia, is very common in India and can make you feel tired, dizzy, or short of breath.

Your haemoglobin is [VALUE] grams per decilitre. The normal range is 13 to 17 for men and 12 to 15 for women.

Eating iron-rich foods like spinach, lentils, and meat can help. Your doctor may also suggest iron tablets if your level is low.
